Gecko Toes Stick Without Glue

Millions of tiny toe hairs split into finer tips. Close contact lets van der Waals interactions hold the foot to a surface. Dry grip comes from shape and scale, not suction or wet glue.

Mechanism: many small contacts, not sticky fluid.
Source: Autumn et al., "Evidence for van der Waals adhesion in gecko setae," PNAS, 2002. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12198184/
